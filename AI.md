## Project 1

### Which tools did you use?

<!-- e.g. ChatGPT, Claude, GitHub Copilot, Midjourney, an image/sound generator, … -->

I used ChatGPT.

### What did you use them for?

<!-- Where did AI help? Ideas, writing/debugging code, creating visual or audio assets, explaining a concept, refactoring, … -->

When I had the idea, I tried at first to code by myself using the past exercises as an example. When I needed help or got stuck, I asked ChatGPT to give me the code and explain every step of it in an easy understandable way.

### How did you use them?

<!-- Include the key prompts you used, or a short summary of the back-and-forth. Be specific enough that someone could follow your process. -->

I usually wrote down the problem that occured during coding and then added "Explain each steps to me clearly and give me the solution in the end".

### What was your own contribution?

<!-- What did YOU decide, change, reject, or build on top of the AI's output? Which creative and technical choices were yours? -->

I decided on the stylistic choices, like the wave pattern, typography, image etc. and the effect it should give, which I changed after the mentoring from a single moving word to the image with rasterized typography.

### Reflection

<!-- One or two sentences: what worked well, what didn't, and what you learned about using AI as a creative tool. -->

It is easy, to just let AI give you the full solution, but I realized that I should use it as a method to explain the code step-by-step in order to better understand it.
